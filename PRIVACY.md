# Stow Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 27, 2026_

Stow is a Mac app that holds files, photos, text and links while you drag them between apps.

## The short version

Stow doesn't collect any information about you, and nothing you put on it leaves your Mac.

## Details

- **No data collection.** Stow has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. It doesn't collect, sell or share personal information, and it doesn't send anything to its developer.
- **No internet connection.** Stow doesn't connect to the internet. Links you save only open if you open them, in your own browser.
- **Your shelf stays on your Mac.** So your items survive a restart, Stow saves the shelf inside its own private folder on your Mac. For files, it saves a reference to where the file lives; it doesn't copy the file. Screenshots, photos and images that arrive without a file of their own are saved in that private folder too. When you remove them from the shelf, they're moved to the Trash.
- **Drags.** Stow slides in when you pick something up because it checks the *kind* of thing being dragged, for example "a file" or "some text". It doesn't look at the content itself unless you drop it on the shelf.
- **Clipboard.** Stow reads the clipboard only when you choose "Add Clipboard Contents to Stow".
- **Sharing.** If you use Share… on an item, macOS's own sharing services send it wherever you choose. Stow isn't involved in that transfer.
